## Intent

A ~30s general-launch film for Dorni, the holding company behind the D2C brands
rewriting human health. Concept chosen from the pitch round: **the immortal
jellyfish**. Open fast on the story of *Turritopsis dohrnii*, the jellyfish that
never dies, then pivot to the thesis in the user's words:

- "AI has eaten software. The world of atoms is yet to be disrupted."
- "Now we have the tools to make brands last forever."
- "We want more functional, healthier and longer human lives through D2C brands
  that will get enhanced by Dorni OS."

Tone: calm confidence, deep-sea, a little playful. Sell (promo), not a site tour.
